# Reconnecting Local Communities: Proposed Remedies for United States v. Google LLC

This project analyzes Google Search's impact on local community connectivity, arguing that its current practices hinder intra-community communication and contribute to the 'Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation' identified by the U.S. Surgeon General (2023). We posit that Google acts more as a gatekeeper than a gateway for local information and propose remedies within the context of *United States v. Google LLC*, based on a case study in South Whidbey, WA. For the complete open-letter to the US Supreme Court please see Remedies_USvGoogleLLC.pdf.
